The old paths

In the next generation Martyn Lloyd-Jones preached at Westminster Chapel in London for thirty years, and the Banner of Truth Trust, founded in 1957, put the Puritans back into print. Reformed Baptist churches took up the Second London Confession of 1689 again.

The editor.

Billy Graham preached to vast crowds on every continent, and in 1974 the Lausanne Congress on World Evangelization, with John Stott as chief drafter of its covenant, bound evangelism and Christian social duty together.

The editor.

Chicago, 1978
