Where Christians live

In 1910 two in three of the world’s Christians lived in Europe. A century later about one in four did, and most lived in the Americas, Africa and Asia.

The editor.

By 2025, on the estimate of the Center for the Study of Global Christianity, about 69 per cent of the world’s Christians lived in the global South.

The editor.
